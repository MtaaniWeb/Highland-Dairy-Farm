About the farm

About Highland Dairy Farm

Highland Dairy Farm is a modern dairy farming enterprise dedicated to producing quality milk and supplying farmers with healthy, productive dairy cattle.

We specialize in carefully selected dairy breeds including Girolando and Jersey cows, helping farmers improve their herds with reliable and high-performing animals.

Through proper feeding, responsible breeding, and professional animal care, we ensure every cow meets the standards our customers expect.

Our mission

To support Kenyan farmers with quality dairy solutions that improve milk production, strengthen livelihoods, and promote sustainable agriculture.
